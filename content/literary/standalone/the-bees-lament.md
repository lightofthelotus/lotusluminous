I hum through fields where flowers fade,
Their colors dimmed by deals man made.
The flowers sigh, their sweetness cold,
Our wings' long labor stole and sold.

They build their towers, steel and stone,
Yet leave the blossoms all alone.
The rivers choke, the skies grow gray,
And still they take, and take away.

But I have seen their gentle hands,
Plant seeds of hope across the lands.
A child who saves a bloom for me,
Restores the song of harmony.

Greed may devour, but love can sow,
The world decides which way to grow.
I buzz, I warn, I plead, I sing,
Choose kindness over everything.
