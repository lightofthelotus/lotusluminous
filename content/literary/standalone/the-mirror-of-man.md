Beneath the stars, a restless flame,
Man seeks both glory and acclaim.
He builds with hands, he breaks with pride,
A heart of love, yet fear inside.

He tends the soil, he scars the land,
He heals with care, destroys with hand.
Compassion blooms, yet envy grows,
The river flows where reason goes.

He dreams of peace, invents the sword,
He prays for truth, yet twists the word.
A paradox of night and day,
Human nature walks its way.

For in each soul, both dark and bright,
The beast and angel share their fight.
And only choice, both kind and true,
Decides the world that man will brew.
